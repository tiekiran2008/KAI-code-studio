# Team Collaboration Guide

## Overview
Antigravity AI supports multi-user collaboration through its robust Team feature. Teams allow you to share workspaces, repositories, and AI memories with colleagues while maintaining strict security constraints.

## Getting Started

### 1. Creating a Team
Navigate to the "Team Collaboration" section in the sidebar and click **Create Team**.
- Provide a clear name for your team.
- You will automatically be assigned the **Owner** role.

### 2. Inviting Members
Go to the **Pending Invitations** tab or click **Invite Member** on the dashboard.
- Enter the colleague's email address.
- Select their appropriate role based on what access they require.
- They will receive an invitation token which they can use to join the team.

### 3. Role Management
Role assignments can be managed on the **Team Members** page.
- As an Owner or Admin, you can upgrade or downgrade member roles.
- Refer to the **Roles & Permissions** tab to see exactly what each role allows.

### 4. Transferring Ownership
If you need to hand over control of the team:
- Go to **Team Settings**.
- Provide the User ID of the new owner.
- Note: You will be automatically downgraded to Admin.
